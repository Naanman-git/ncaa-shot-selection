# Presentation script

Eleven slides, approximately 7-8 minutes.

## 1. NCAA shot selection

This deck presents the frozen NCAA Division I men’s basketball analysis. It is a descriptive historical study, not a university or team product.

## 2. Study population

The cohort is reconciled native shot data joined to team boxes; counts are included coverage, not a claim of complete NCAA coverage.

## 3. Chronological study design

Training seasons fit candidates; 2024-25 selects the model; 2025-26 is the final fixed test.

## 4. Prespecified hypotheses

Four prespecified hypotheses connect distance, expected value, shrinkage and held-out model comparison to the project blueprint.

## 5. Held-out probability performance

Scores are held-out Brier and log loss. The history model uses only earlier dates within a season.

## 6. Where shots occur and what they are worth

Cells show recorded chart coordinates with sparse cells omitted. Point values and zones are geometry-inferred by the upstream adapter.

## 7. Calibration and uncertainty

Calibration and game bootstrap quantify sampling behavior, but do not create causal certainty.

## 8. Constrained case study · Ohio State Buckeyes

The case study uses a fixed-mix constrained scenario with shrinkage and explicit support gates.

## 9. Sensitivity to the per-zone limit

Sensitivity shows how analyst-set movement limits change the conditional result.

## 10. Prospective validation package

The prospective package freezes the design, supplies an anonymous template and analyzes new attempts once they exist.

## 11. What this study supports

The product supports reproducible exploration and clearly separates historical evidence from a future strategy experiment.
